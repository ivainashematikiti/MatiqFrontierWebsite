Frequently asked questions

Before you request a quotation.

A few useful details to help you understand how enquiries are handled.

Do you only supply schools and offices?

No. We accept enquiries across hardware and building supplies, agriculture, ICT, industrial and workshop supplies, cleaning and hygiene, warehousing, transport and logistics, bulk projects and other sourcing needs.

Do you keep every product in stock?

No. We operate on a sourcing and procurement basis. Availability, supplier lead times and pricing are checked for each request and confirmed in the quotation.

Can you arrange delivery or storage?

We can assess and coordinate suitable third-party transport or warehousing arrangements for an agreed requirement, subject to provider availability, location, cost and terms.

What should I include in my enquiry?

Include item descriptions, specifications or brands where relevant, quantities, your organisation, delivery town and when you need the items. Attachments can be discussed directly over WhatsApp.

Do I have to order after receiving a quotation?

No. Review the quotation and its terms first. An order proceeds only when the required details and conditions are agreed.

Which areas do you serve?

We welcome enquiries from across Zimbabwe. Feasibility, delivery options and costs are confirmed based on the specific location and order.
